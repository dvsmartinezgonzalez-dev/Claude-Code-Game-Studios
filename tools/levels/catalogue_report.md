## The problem that was fixed

The previous `Resources/levels.json` held **30 levels that were really only 7
distinct puzzles** — the other 23 were the same board under color relabeling and/or
tube reordering (a single "cyclic-shift / staircase" template). Several `par_moves`
were *below* the optimal solution, making the 3-star target impossible, and the
curve was non-monotonic (4-color levels were easier than 3-color ones because an
extra buffer had been added).

| Old catalogue | 30 | **7** | 23 |
| New catalogue | 50 | **50** | 0 |

Old structural-equivalence groups (same puzzle in disguise):
`{1,4} {2,3} {5} {6,7,8,9} {10..15} {16..25} {26..30}`.

## How variety is guaranteed now

Every level is a distinct full-packed random deal, proven solvable by an optimal
A* solver, filtered to a unique canonical signature (no relabel/reorder twin) and
placed on a target difficulty percentile. The staircase template is explicitly
rejected. Difficulty rises from **scramble structure** and gentle color/depth
growth — never from a repeated pattern. Two full buffers are the baseline for all
of 1–50; **single buffers and restricted buffers are reserved for 51+**, so level
50 sits at roughly one third of the eventual ceiling of the planned ~150-level game.
